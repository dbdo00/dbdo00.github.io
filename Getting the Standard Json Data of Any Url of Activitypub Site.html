Getting the Standard Json Data of Any Url of Activitypub Site

Show Me the Code

I just learned how to retrieve the standard json format data of a activitypub status/profile.

In the straightforward manner, let me show you the code:

curl -X GET <any url of a activitypub site> -H 'Accept: application/ld+json; profile="https://www.w3.org/ns/activitystreams"'

That’s pretty much it!

How I Learned About This

There’s nothing complicated about this. It is just a simple standard specified in the activitypub’s document. But I feel strange why this is not mentioned enough. I have seen people complaining that mastodon’s status page does not support browser without functionality to run Javascript.

I am inspired by people trying to understand activitypub and develop upon it, without blindly accepting Mastodon as the overly dominating platform that speaks the language of activitypub protocol.

It is interesting that every platforms supporting activitypub allows people to communicate regardless of which specific website they choose.

Concepts

Suppose you are given a handle like user@activitypub.example. You do not know yet what formtat of url the site uses, but you know it supports activitypub. The method to find the url(s) that this handle refers to on the site activitypub.example is through the url

/.well-known/webfinger?resource=acct:user@activitypub.example

Enter the command

curl  https://mas.to/.well-known/webfinger?user@activitypub.example | jq

will tell the aliases (for example, user@activitypub.example) and the link (e.g. html page for human to read, the application/activity+json format for other AP platforms to fetch).

This is also standard and adapted by every activitypub platforms I tested. Strangely, webfinger does not seem to be mentioned in activity pub’s document, nor in the activity stream’s https://www.w3.org/TR/activitystreams-core/#urls
. But it indeed seems to be very universal. see WebFinger

After knowing url, you can retrieve the standard data and find where the outbox is.

The outbox is discovered through the outbox property of an actor’s profile.
