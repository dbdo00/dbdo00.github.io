When software breaks

Value of failed softwares



2026-07-08 It is less talked about broken softwares than those that suceeded. Sucess is valuable but failure is more common and “realistic”. Despite being useful as a material of why the ‘right design’ is right, the failure cases tend to be hidden or forgetten, and only sucessful ones are recorded and well-known. It should be of interest to analyze which algorithms, say, language models, failed, and how they differ from the current ones in use.

The feedback in grand engineering
