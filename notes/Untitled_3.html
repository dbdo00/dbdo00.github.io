The slice angles form a geometric sequence $a_n$, with common ratio $r = 1.22$, and the initial term $a_0=10$.

Let the number of slices be $n$. Then

$$10(\frac{r^n - 1}{r - 1}) = 10 ( 1 + r + \ldots + r^{n-1} ) =\ \rm total\ angle$$

Here the total angle is of that of the circular cake, which is 360 degree.

Rearrangement gives $n = \frac{\ln [ (r - 1 ) ({\rm \ total\ angle }/10) + 1 ]}{\ln r} = 11.0047... \approx 11$

In the slices, the taken ones are the 4th and 8th.

The angle of the 4th: $a_3 = 10 \cdot r ^3$.

THe anlge of the 8th: $a_7 = 10 \cdot r^4$.

The total angles taken:
$${\rm taken\ } = 10 \times (r^3 + r^7) \approx 147.50209$$

So the remaining area is:

$$\pi r^2 \times \left ( 1 - {\rm taken\ } / 360\right) \approx 65$$.
