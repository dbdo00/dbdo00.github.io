  • Q: What is the largest number of points on the plain, each two of which have the equal $\ell_1$ distance?
    • If $x \in B(y)$, then $B(x) \cap B(y) \leq 2$. Where $B()$ is the $\ell_1$ ball of the plane.
      • If $x,\ y$ have \ell 1 distance $d$, what is the number of other points having the equal distance from $x$ and $y$?
      • The answer is $2$, as the inequality above asserts.
    • So, the maximal number of pairwise $\ell_1$-equidistant points on $\mathbb R^2$ is $\boxed 4$.
