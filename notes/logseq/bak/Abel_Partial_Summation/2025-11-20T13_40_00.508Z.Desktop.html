Partial Summation

Questions

Find the asymptotic expression for the following functions: $\sum_{n< x}n \log(n)$, $\sum_{n< x} \log(n)$, $\sum_{n< x} \frac{1}{n}$.

Sometimes we want to find the growth trend of the summations that have no closed form formulae.
Instead of the summation, we integrate it and see how much error we get. The integration may give a simpler expression that is easier to take limits.

Partial Summation

Let $\{a_n\}$ be a sequence of complex numbers and $f(t)$ a differential function for $t\geq 0$. Set $A(x) = \sum_{n\leq x}a_n$. Then

$$\sum_{n\leq x}a_n f(x) = A(x)f(x) - \int^x_1 A(t)f^\prime(x)dt$$

Proof Note $a_n = A(n) -A(n-1)$.

$$\begin{align*} & \sum_{n < x} (A(n) &- &A(n-1)) f(n) &\\ = & \sum_{n \leq x} A(n)f(n) &- &\sum_{n\leq x} A(n-1) f(n)& \\ = & A(x)f(x) &+ &\sum_{n \leq x - 1} A(n)f(x + 1) &\\ =& A(x)f(x) &- &\int^x_1A(t)f^\prime(t)dt &\end{align*}$$
