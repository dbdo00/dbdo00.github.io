Let $e_1 = 1,\, e_2=x,\, e_3=x^2$. The invariant subspaces wrt $\Delta$ of $V$ are
$W_1={\rm span}(e_1), W_2={\rm span}(e_1, e_2), W_3= {\rm span}(e_1, e_2, e_3)$

Proof. Let $W$ be an invariant space of $W$ wrt $\Delta$.
Suppose $W_j$ is the maximal subspace in $\{W_k\}_{k=1,2,3}$ that contains $W$. Then we can show that $W$ contains $W_j$ as well. So $W=W_j$.
