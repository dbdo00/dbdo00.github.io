Integrals involving Absolute Values

last updated: Nov 18, 2025

You want to simplify the function, not just the integral region.
For trig functions, consider the identities that reduce the sum of sins and cos into one trig functions.

$$\int_{x^2 + y^2 \leq 1}|x^2 -y^2|dxdy$$
