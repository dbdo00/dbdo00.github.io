quadratic_space

last updated: Sep 17, 2025

Theory

A Quadratic form is a function $Q\colon V \to K$ such that

  • $Q(ax) = a^2 Q(x)$
  • $(x,y) \mapsto Q(x)Q(y) -Q(x) - Q(y)$ is a bilinear form.

We deote by $(x,y)$ the bilinear form $\frac{1}{2}(Q(x)Q(y) -Q(x) - Q(y))$.
The radical of a quadratic subspace $W$ is $W^\perp ={ v \in V | \forall w\in W,, (w,v) =0 }$.

A space $V$ is non-degenerate if $V^\perp = {0}$.

Exercises

  1. Let $V$ be a non-degenerate space. Then $V= \text{dim} W^\perp + \text{dim} W$.

Solution. Consider the natural map $f\colon V\to V^$. Recall that $V \cong V^$, so they have the same
dimension. So, $f$ is surjective if and only if $f$ is injective. Since $V$ is non-degenerate, $f$ is injective,
whence $f$ is surjective. Since the restriction of a linear functional $v* : V \to V*$ to $W$ is a surjection
$V*\to W*$, composition of this restriction with $f$ gives a surjection $g\colon V \to W^$. Hence
$$\text{dim} V = \text{dim} \ker g + \text{dim} W^ = \text{dim} W^{\perp} + \text{dim} W.$$ Q.E.D.
