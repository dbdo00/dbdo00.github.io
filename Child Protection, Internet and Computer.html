Child Protection, Internet and Computer



2026-06-27 Europe is trying to stop children from having access to social media. It seems that the louder voice is opposed to this policy, from the ground of privicy. Perhaps these adults are raised too well or are too lucky to understand how social media can negatively influence a child.

China has the opposite policy: children cannot play games too long, but they are not restricted on social media using.
