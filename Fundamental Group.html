Fundamental Group



2025-11-29 This post will outline the fundamentals of theory of fundamental group.

There is a homotopy between pp and qq. This implies there is a continuous moving family of paths between p(x)p(x) and q(x)q(x) for every xx.

We wonder: Are p* and q* (the homomorphism induced by p and q$) the same ? The answer is affirmative, up to a change of basepoints.

When the fundamental group based on x0x_0 and x1x_1 are Isomorphic?

It is the case when the two points are path connected.
